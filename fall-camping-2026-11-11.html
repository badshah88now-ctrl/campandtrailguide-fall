Fall Camping: Layering, Gear and Shorter Days

Fall is one of the best seasons to camp: the crowds thin out, the bugs fade, and the trails show their autumn colours. But the season also brings shorter days and bigger temperature swings — a warm afternoon can turn into a cold night fast. Fall camping rewards campers who plan for the cold and pack with layers in mind.

Layering is the core skill. Start with a moisture-wicking base layer, add an insulating mid-layer like fleece or down, and finish with a windproof and waterproof shell. You will take layers off while you hike and put them back on at camp, so choose pieces that pack small. A warm hat and gloves weigh almost nothing and make a big difference after sunset — most heat escapes from an uncovered head.

Your sleep system matters more in fall than in summer. Check your sleeping bag's temperature rating against the expected overnight low for your area, and bring a sleeping pad with enough insulation to keep ground cold away from your body. A bag liner adds a few degrees of warmth for very little weight. Before you leave, test your setup in the garden on a cool night — better to find out at home than at 2 a.m. in the woods.

Shorter days change how you plan. Sunset comes early, so arrive at camp with time to pitch the tent, gather firewood, and cook before dark. Carry a headlamp with fresh batteries and a spare, and keep it in an easy pocket. A campfire becomes the centre of the evening in fall, but check local fire rules first — some areas ban fires during dry spells, and a camp stove is the reliable backup.

Fall weather can turn quickly, so watch the forecast and be ready to change plans. Rain is common, and wet leaves make trails slippery, so pack waterproof boots and a rain shell even if the morning looks clear. Food tastes better in cool weather, and a warm drink at the end of the day is one of camping's simple pleasures — plan meals that are hearty and easy to cook in one pot.

With the right layers and a realistic schedule, fall camping is comfortable and rewarding.
